About Trailers 4 U

Trailers 4 U is a full-service trailer dealership based at 103 N Locust St in Frankfort, Kansas, serving ranchers, farmers, and haulers across the region. We carry stock, horse, flatbed, and utility trailers, along with custom builds, from trusted dealers including Merritt, Travalong, H&H, and BBK.

Whether you're hauling cattle to town, horses to a show, equipment between fields, or building a custom rig for a specific job, we'll help you find the right trailer — new, in stock, or built to your spec.
