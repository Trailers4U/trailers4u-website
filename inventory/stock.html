Stock Trailers

We carry stock trailers from two different builders — Merritt and Travalong — built two different ways. Both are heavy-duty, high-quality trailers; the difference is in construction.

Merritt Stock Trailers

Cattle Baron & Lariat gooseneck lines

Merritt's gooseneck trailers use a steel frame with aluminum everywhere else — walls, roof, and posts — built around Merritt's punch-side siding, a welded-in floor on 3" steel cross-members, and reinforced corners designed to handle years of road twist.

  • Models Cattle Baron, Lariat
  • Common lengths 20', 24', 28', 32' (tri-axle)
  • Interior size 84" wide (7') × 80" tall
  • Construction Steel frame, aluminum walls, roof & posts
  • Standard features Punch-side siding, diamond-plate welded floor, snap-back gate latch, torsion axles, electric brakes

Travalong Stock Trailers

Standard, Outside Skin, 7.5' Wide, Bumper Hitch, Advantage & Rancher Gooseneck

Travalong's livestock trailers come in six builds: the Standard Gooseneck; the Outside Skin Gooseneck, which adds an outer galvannealed steel skin — double-lined with 16-gauge galvannealed steel and caulked seams — for extra weather protection; the 7.5' Wide Standard, a gooseneck-only model with extra interior width and lengths up to 36'; the Bumper Hitch, a compact 14'–18' bumper-pull trailer for smaller loads; the Advantage, a drop-gooseneck model with a wedge nose, G90 galvanized inside siding, and a single-axle 16' option alongside tandem-axle 20' and 24' lengths; and the Rancher Gooseneck, an all-around model with bus windows in the nose, undercoated interior walls, and a solid rear gate with slam latch.

  • Models Standard Gooseneck, Outside Skin Gooseneck, 7.5' Wide Standard, Bumper Hitch, Advantage, Rancher Gooseneck
  • Hitch types Gooseneck (all except Bumper Hitch); bumper-pull (Bumper Hitch)
  • Common lengths 16'–32' gooseneck; up to 36' (7.5' Wide); 14', 16', 18' (Bumper Hitch); 16', 20', 24' (Advantage)
  • Interior size 80" wide (6'8") standard; 90" wide (7.5') on the Wide model
  • GVWR 7,000 lbs single-axle / 14,000 lbs tandem (Advantage, Rancher); 10,160 – 12,000 lbs (Bumper Hitch); 14,000 – 21,000 lbs (Standard/Outside Skin); up to 22,500 lbs tri-axle (7.5' Wide)
  • Construction All-steel frame; Outside Skin model adds galvannealed steel outer skin; Advantage uses G90 galvanized inside siding; Rancher has undercoated interior walls
  • Standard features Torsion axles with electric brakes, breakaway battery box; sliding rear gate with slam & butterfly latch on most models, solid rear gate with slam latch on Rancher
