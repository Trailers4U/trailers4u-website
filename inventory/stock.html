Stock Trailers

We carry stock trailers from two different builders — Merritt and Travalong — built two different ways. Both are heavy-duty, high-quality trailers; the difference is in construction.

Merritt Stock Trailers

Cattle Baron (cattle/livestock) & Lariat (horse)

Merritt's two gooseneck lines share punch-side siding, and aluminum walls and roof — but they're built for different jobs. The Cattle Baron is a livestock hauler; the Lariat is Merritt's horse trailer, with a tack room and flat floor in place of the Cattle Baron's diamond-plate deck.

  • Models Cattle Baron (cattle/livestock), Lariat (horse)
  • Interior size 84" wide (7') × 80" tall
  • Construction Steel frame, aluminum walls, roof & posts
Cattle Baron Cattle & livestock · 20′–32′

Merritt's livestock-focused gooseneck. Lengths: 20′, 24′, 28′, 32′ tri-axle.

  • Diamond-plate ribbed floor welded to 3″ steel cross-members
  • Extruded winter posts for panel support
  • Punch-side siding (Merritt-original design)
  • Single-sheet aluminum roof, riveted & adhesive-bonded
  • Reinforced aluminum-trailer corners
  • Snap-Back gate latching system
  • Front & rear self-draining fender guard steps
  • Rear full-swing gate safety catch
  • Tapered fender wells
  • Segmented rear skid plate
  • Double tail lights & top rear load lights
  • Torsion axles, electric brakes with breakaway kit
Lariat Horse · 20′–28′, 7′ or 8′ wide

Merritt's horse trailer. Lengths: 20×7, 24×7, 28×7, 24×8, 28×8.

  • Lockable tack room with 10 bridle hooks, 4 saddle racks & blanket bar
  • Flat aluminum floor (.157″), suited to rubber matting
  • Sliding nose windows, both road & curbside, for ventilation
  • Punch-side siding, same as Cattle Baron
  • Single-sheet aluminum roof, riveted & adhesive-bonded
  • Rear full-swing gate safety catch
  • Segmented rear skid plate
  • LED interior & tack room lighting
  • Torsion axles, electric brakes with breakaway kit

Travalong Stock Trailers

Standard, Outside Skin, 7.5' Wide, Bumper Hitch, Advantage & Rancher Gooseneck

Travalong's livestock trailers come in six builds, all-steel frame construction, each with a different nose, gate, or sizing setup. Expand a model below to see its features.

  • Hitch types Gooseneck (all except Bumper Hitch); bumper-pull (Bumper Hitch)
  • Interior size 80" wide (6'8") standard; 90" wide (7.5') on the Wide model
  • Axles Tandem axle standard on every length; tri-axle on the longer Standard and 7.5' Wide models
  • Construction All-steel frame throughout
Standard Gooseneck 16′–32′

GVWR 14,000 lbs (16′–24′), 21,000 lbs tri-axle (28′, 32′).

  • Fold-down nose gate
  • Sliding rear gate, slam & butterfly latch
  • 42″ sloped, tapered nose
  • Treated wood floor
  • Torsion axles, electric brakes
  • Escape door, passenger side, 42″ opening
  • Breakaway battery box with charger/tester
  • Crossmembers & uprights on 16″ centers
Outside Skin Gooseneck Galvannealed steel skin

Same base as the Standard Gooseneck, plus an outer weatherproofing skin.

  • Outside galvannealed steel skin
  • Double-lined with 16-gauge galvannealed steel
  • Caulked outside seams
  • Same nose, gate, axle & floor spec as Standard
7.5' Wide Standard Up to 36′, gooseneck only

90″ wide interior (vs. 80″ standard). GVWR up to 22,500 lbs tri-axle at 36′.

  • Lengths: 16′, 20′, 24′, 28′, 32′, 36′
  • 28′ and larger have frame truss & boxed stem
  • 32′ available only with 1/2 nose 10″ channel drop gooseneck
  • Same gate & axle features as Standard Gooseneck
Bumper Hitch 14′–18′, bumper-pull

GVWR 10,160–12,000 lbs depending on length.

  • Compact bumper-pull hitch (no gooseneck)
  • Topwind 5K jack with removable foot
  • Tapered nose, Gator Hyde gravel guard
  • Sliding rear gate, slam & butterfly latch
  • Torsion axles, electric brakes
Advantage 16′, 20′, 24′ · tandem axle

GVWR 14,000 lbs. All lengths come standard on tandem axles.

  • Drop gooseneck with wedge nose
  • G90 galvanized inside siding (57″)
  • Travaluscent roof
  • Sliding rear gate, slam & butterfly latch
  • Drop-leg dual pin spring retraction jack
Rancher Gooseneck Bus windows, 16′–32′ · tandem axle

GVWR 14,000 lbs. All lengths come standard on tandem axles.

  • Two 14″×34″ bus windows in the nose
  • Undercoated interior walls
  • Solid rear gate with slam latch (not sliding)
  • 42″ tapered nose
  • Aluminum treadplate gravel guard

Nothing currently in stock in the Travalong line — call to check availability or ask about ordering one built to spec.
