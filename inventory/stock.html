Travalong

Standard, Gooseneck Heavyweight, Outside Skin, 7.5' Wide, Bumper Hitch, Advantage & Rancher Gooseneck

Travalong stock trailers come in seven builds, all with all-steel frame construction.

  • Hitch types Gooseneck, bumper-pull
  • Interior width 80" standard; 90" on 7.5' Wide
  • Axles Tandem standard; tri-axle on longer models
  • Construction All-steel frame
Standard Gooseneck 16'–32'

Lengths from 16' to 32'. GVWR is 14,000 lbs on the 16' through 24', and 21,000 lbs on the tri-axle 28' and 32'.

  • Fold-down nose gate
  • Sliding rear gate with slam and butterfly latch
  • 42" sloped, tapered nose
  • Treated wood floor
  • Torsion axles with electric brakes
  • 42" escape door, passenger side
  • Breakaway battery box
  • Crossmembers and uprights on 16" centers
Gooseneck Heavyweight 6'8" × 6'8" · 16'–32'

Named for its heavier build: crossmembers and 1"x2" uprights on 12" centers, versus 16" on the Standard. Lengths are 16', 20', 24', 28', and 32'.

  • 6'8" wide and 6'8" tall
  • Tandem axle on 16'–24'
  • 28' and 32' step up to tri-axle with triple 7,000# axles
  • 16' and 20': one solid interior gate
  • 24', 28' and 32': two interior gates
  • Crossmembers and 1"x2" uprights on 12" centers
Outside Skin Gooseneck Steel outer skin

Built on the same base as the Standard Gooseneck, with an outer galvannealed steel skin added.

  • Outer galvannealed steel skin
  • Double-lined with 16-gauge galvannealed steel
  • Caulked seams
  • Same base features as the Standard Gooseneck
7.5' Wide Standard 90" wide · 16'–36'

A wider build with a 90" interior, in lengths from 16' to 36'. GVWR reaches 22,500 lbs on the tri-axle 36'.

  • 90" interior width
  • 16' to 36' lengths
  • GVWR up to 22,500 lbs (36' tri-axle)
  • 28' and longer have a frame truss and boxed stem
  • 32' is only available with the half-nose 10" channel drop gooseneck
Bumper Hitch 14', 16' & 18'

A bumper-pull stock trailer, with no gooseneck, in 14', 16', and 18' lengths. GVWR runs 10,160 to 12,000 lbs.

  • Bumper-pull hitch
  • GVWR 10,160–12,000 lbs
  • Topwind 5K jack
  • Tapered nose
  • Gator Hyde gravel guard
  • Sliding rear gate
  • Torsion axles with electric brakes
Advantage Drop gooseneck · 16', 20', 24'

A drop gooseneck with a wedge nose, in 16', 20', and 24' lengths. GVWR is 14,000 lbs, and every length comes standard on tandem axles.

  • Drop gooseneck, wedge nose
  • GVWR 14,000 lbs, tandem axles on all lengths
  • G90 galvanized inside siding (57")
  • Travaluscent roof
  • Sliding rear gate
  • Drop-leg dual pin spring retraction jack
Rancher Gooseneck 16'–32'

Lengths from 16' to 32'. GVWR is 14,000 lbs, and every length comes standard on tandem axles.

  • Two 14"x34" bus windows in the nose
  • Solid rear gate with slam latch
  • 42" tapered nose
  • Aluminum treadplate gravel guard
  • GVWR 14,000 lbs, tandem axles on all lengths
