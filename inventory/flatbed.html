Travalong Flatbed Trailers

Full Width Lowboy, Deckover, Tiltbed & Skidsteer

All-steel frame construction across the line. Weight ratings, deck heights, and axle configurations vary by model — expand each one below for details.

  • Construction All-steel frame
  • Deck heights 22" – 36" by model
  • Axles 7,000# standard; upgrades on Deckover
Full Width Lowboy Bumper-pull or gooseneck · 20'–28'

Available as a bumper-pull or gooseneck, in 20', 24', 26', and 28' lengths — each including a 4' dovetail (so a "20'" is really a 16' flat deck plus a 4' dovetail, and so on up the line). Standard on 7,000# axles.

  • 83.5" between the wheel wells
  • 102" wide measured outside the drive-over fenders
  • Two full-width flip-over ramps
  • 28.5" deck height
  • 7,000# axles
  • Bumper-pull or gooseneck hitch
Deckover Flatbed 7,000# & 10,000# · 21'–40'

Offered in two weight classes. The 7,000# rating runs 21', 23', 25', 27', 29', 31', 33', and 35' long; the 10,000# rating runs 25', 27', 29', 31', 33', 35', and 40' long. Both sit at a 36" deck height.

  • 36" deck height, both ratings
  • 7,000#: standard tandem axle; third axle available
  • 10,000#: upgradeable to 2/12,000# axles
  • 10,000#: optional triple 10,000# or 12,000# dually axles
  • Air brakes available (10,000#)
  • 3" frame truss add-on available
  • 4'x6' deck-over-the-nose option
  • Underbody toolbox option
  • Extra torque tube option
Tiltbed 11° tilt · 16'–26'

Seven tiltbed configurations, every one on 2/7,000# axles with an 11-degree tilt angle. Standard as a bumper-pull; can be upgraded to a gooseneck.

  • 16' — full deck tilts
  • 20' — 16' tilt + 4' stationary deck
  • 22' — 16' tilt + 4' stationary deck
  • 20' — full 20' tilt
  • 24' — 20' tilt + 4' stationary deck
  • 24' — 16' tilt + 8' stationary deck
  • 26' — 20' tilt + 6' stationary deck
  • 7,000# axles (x2), all lengths
  • D-rings available
  • Electric winch available (12,000# or 15,000# load)
  • Fork pockets available
Skidsteer Trailer 82.5" wide · 12'–22'

Standard as a bumper-pull, with a gooseneck available for an additional charge. Comes in 12', 14', 16', 18', 20', and 22' lengths on 7,000# axles.

  • 22" deck height
  • 82.5" wide between the fenders
  • Two 6' heavy-duty stand-up ramps with drop leg
  • 7,000# axles
  • Gooseneck upgrade available
  • Fork pockets available
